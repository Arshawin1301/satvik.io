About Us

Our Mission: Pure Milk, Honest Farming

Our journey began with a troubling realization: the widespread adulteration in dairy products. Motivated by a sense of responsibility, we left our corporate jobs to start a dairy farm committed to one simple goal: delivering genuinely pure milk.

Starting with our first cow, our herd has grown to a healthy mix of Gir and Housten breeds. Every step is guided by our commitment to transparency, quality, and the well-being of our customers and our animals.

History

Our Journey

2024

Journey Was Started

We began our journey with a deep dive into the dairy industry, researching the alarming prevalence of adulteration in milk and milk products. During this period, we engaged with local communities to understand consumer mindsets.

Breed Research

Our focus shifted to understanding the nutritional value of different cow breeds. Through extensive research, we learned about the superior health benefits of milk from Gir and Housten cows. Gir cow milk, in particular, is renowned for its A2 protein and easier digestibility.

2024

2024

First Cows Purchased

With our vision clear, we purchased our first Gir and Housten cows. This marked the official start of our dairy operations. We began supplying pure, unadulterated milk to households in Vapi and Valsad, ensuring our customers received the health benefits of high-quality A2 milk.

Growth & Expansion

Our efforts have quickly gained traction. Today, we are proud to be delivering over 100 liters of milk daily, serving a growing community that values authenticity and health.

2025

2025

Future Ambitions

Our ambition is to scale up and reach 1,000 liters per day by the end of the year, making pure, nutritious milk accessible to even more families.

Our Founders

Kavish Jain

Co-Founder

With over 14 years of experience in logistics and supply chain management, Kavish Jain founded Satvik Dairy Farm with a vision to revolutionize the dairy industry. His journey began when he identified the pressing issue of adulteration in dairy products and decided to take action. Leaving behind a successful corporate career, he established Satvik Dairy Farm to provide pure, unadulterated milk to families.

Uday Rathod

Co-Founder

With over 14 years of rich experience in supervision, manufacturing, sales, and management, Uday Rathod is a driving force behind Satvik. Before co-founding the company, he led a major unit for one of the country's largest organizations, where he honed his expertise in streamlining processes and ensuring operational excellence.

Troubled by the widespread issue of food adulteration, particularly in the dairy industry, Uday was inspired to create a brand that prioritizes purity and transparency. He joined his childhood friends to establish Satvik, where his operational expertise and unwavering integrity form the backbone of the company’s mission to deliver real, non-adulterated milk to every customer.
