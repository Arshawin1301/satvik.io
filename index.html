Our Breeds

Meet Our Herd

Gir Cow

Gir cow milk is highly valued for its A2 beta-casein protein, which is often considered easier to digest than the A1 protein found in conventional milk, potentially reducing digestive discomfort. It is a rich source of essential nutrients, including calcium for strong bones, and contains Omega-3 fatty acids that support heart health.

Holstein Friesian (HF) Cow

Holstein Friesian (HF) milk is known for its consistent quality and affordability, thanks to the breed's high milk yield. It provides a balanced profile of essential nutrients, including protein, calcium, and vitamins, making it a reliable and nutritious choice for daily consumption by families.

Buffalo

Buffalo milk is renowned for its rich and creamy texture, making it a preferred choice for dairy products like paneer, ghee, and yogurt. It is higher in fat, protein, and calcium compared to cow's milk, offering a dense nutritional profile that supports bone health and provides sustained energy.

Organic Fertilizer

Vermicompost

What is Vermicompost?

Vermicompost is a nutrient-rich, organic fertilizer and soil conditioner produced through the decomposition of organic matter by earthworms. These worms consume farm waste, breaking it down into highly fertile castings that are packed with essential minerals, beneficial microbes, and plant growth hormones.

Advantages of Vermicompost:

  • Enriches soil with essential nutrients and beneficial microorganisms.
  • Improves soil structure, aeration, and water retention capacity.
  • Enhances seed germination, plant growth, and crop yield.
  • 100% organic, eco-friendly, and free from harmful chemicals.
